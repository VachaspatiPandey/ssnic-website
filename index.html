About SSNIC

A School Built on Purpose

Savitri Shiksha Niketan Inter College (SSNIC) was established in 2004 under the aegis of Savitri Shikshan Sansthan — at a time when access to quality education in this area was limited. This institution was established to change that.

Our commitment has always been simple: to give every student who walks through our doors the best education and guidance we possibly can.

Recognised by the Uttar Pradesh Board of Secondary Education, we offer education from Nursery through Class XII in Barahua, Sangipur, Pratapgarh.

Principal's Message

Dear Parents and Students,

It gives me immense pride to welcome you to Savitri Shiksha Niketan Inter College (SSNIC). Since its establishment in 2004, under the aegis of Savitri Shikshan Sansthan — at a time when access to quality education in this area was limited, this institution was established to change that. Our commitment has always been simple: to give every student who walks through our doors the best education and guidance we possibly can.

At SSNIC, we believe true education goes beyond textbooks. Discipline and strong moral values form the foundation on which we shape every child.

As Principal, I take personal responsibility in ensuring that every student here grows not just academically, but as a disciplined, grounded, and capable individual. We work closely with parents because we believe a child's growth is a shared journey between school and home.

I invite you to be a part of this journey with us.

Managing Director's Message

I am not an outsider to this school. I sat in these classrooms myself.

Savitri Shikshan Sansthan was built on one honest intention — that every child in this area deserves a real education. Having been a student here, I understand what that means, not just as a responsibility, but personally.

My job as Managing Director is simple: to make sure this institution keeps up with that intention. We are not chasing recognition. We are focused on doing right by every student who walks through our gates.

That is enough of a mission for me.

Vachaspati Pandey
Managing Director, Savitri Shikshan Sansthan

Our Approach

Education for Today's Era

"The most important thing we can give a student is not just information — it's the ability to think."

Our teaching goes beyond traditional textbook learning. We focus on practical understanding, modern skills, and the kind of thinking ability that today's world actually demands — alongside the discipline and values that have always been at our core.

We believe that once a student truly learns how to think for themselves, everything else follows. They become capable of shaping their own path, solving their own problems, and building a life on their own terms.

This is what we aim for — students who are not just educated, but genuinely capable.
